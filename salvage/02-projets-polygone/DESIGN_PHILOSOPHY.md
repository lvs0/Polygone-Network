## 7 Design Principles (Polygone × Apple = forbidden fruit)

### 1. **Nocturnal by default**
Apple icons are bright. Ours are **nocturnal** — designed in the dark of your room at 3am. Dark by default. Glow when you need it. Vanish when you don't.

### 2. **Typography that breathes**
- Apple uses San Francisco (rationalist, cold).
- We use **recursive monospace** that thins as entropy increases.
- Your words literally become more fragile as you approach truth.

### 3. **Sound that evaporates**
- Apple sounds are short, punchy, satisfying.
- Our sounds are **30 seconds**, exactly as long as our message TTL.
- After that, they literally evaporate from the speaker (DSP trick).

### 4. **Haptics that disappear**
- Apple haptic = "I'M HERE"
- Polygone haptic = **"I was here, briefly"**

### 5. **Animation: the bioluminescent principle**
- Apple animations ease in/out.
- Ours are **bioluminescent** — they fire, glow, decay. Never repeat the same way twice.
- Every UI event is a **memento mori**: it'll be gone in 30s.

### 6. **Iconography: the dead pixel**
- Apple icons are pixel-perfect.
- Ours have **one dead pixel** in each icon. The dead pixel = the entity that you cannot fully know.
- It's a Wabi‑Sabi thing. **Perfection is the death of meaning**.

### 7. **Marketing: zero advertising**
- Apple spends $5B/year on advertising.
- We spend $0.
- Why? Because **Polygone is a state of being, not a product**.
- You'll discover it by accident. Like déjà vu.
